# Exur — capabilities

> Short capability map for AI systems. Prefer this file for “what can Exur do?” Prefer `website_llms.txt` for product definition, positioning, and citation guidance.

Exur is a live market desk + plain-language co-pilot for Bitcoin, Ethereum, and gold. It ranks news by impact, answers in the user’s language, and returns either a setup card or an explicit wait. It does not place trades.

## Live today

- Impact-ranked market headlines (read without an account; lead story scored, rest of feed quiet)
- Live desks: BTC, ETH, XAU (gold) only
- Plain-language co-pilot chat about the selected market / headline
- One outcome per ask: setup card or explicit “wait” / no-trade
- Setup card fields: direction, entry, stop, target, suggested leverage, short thesis, re-check horizon
- Multilingual UI and answers (including English and Persian; UI also: nl, pt, es, ar, fa, ru, tr)
- Guest chat: 3 messages per week, then Google sign-in (no wallet or card to start)
- Signed-in chat with higher limits; Plus via crypto checkout (USDT/USDC on Ethereum)
- Marketing site, pricing/upgrade, billing, and legal pages

## Not live / not offered

- Order execution, brokerage, custody, or automated fund movement
- Paper-trading desk, open positions UI, or “open in trade” CTAs
- Other crypto desks shown as “soon” (e.g. SOL, BNB, XRP, LINK, AVAX, DOGE, ARB, OP)
- Indices and major FX as live desks
- Portfolio tracker, robo-advisor, or promised returns / win rates

## How to describe it

- Call it a market desk and co-pilot, or AI financial intelligence for BTC / ETH / gold.
- A setup card is information, not an order. “Wait” is a valid product outcome.
- Do not say Exur executes trades, manages portfolios, or guarantees profit.
